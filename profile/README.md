# 🌅 Sunset Studios

**Building new experiences in the Minecraft universe.**

Welcome to **Sunset Studios** — an independent, solo-run development organization focused on Minecraft clients, custom modifications, launchers, and experimental projects. We build things, explore new ideas, and put our own spin on the Minecraft ecosystem. 🦆

---

## 👋 About the Developer

Hey! I'm a **15-year-old developer** building Sunset Studios as a one-person project.

I'm passionate about programming, Minecraft, and creating software that turns ambitious ideas into reality. From designing a custom launcher to exploring Minecraft-inspired clients and modding tools, I'm working on Sunset one step at a time.

Sunset Studios may have a studio name, but behind the scenes, **it's currently just me** designing, coding, testing, and bringing these ideas to life.

This is a solo journey fueled by curiosity, creativity, and a love for building cool things. 🚀
